# Reward policy

This workspace is a local screening tool. It does not claim GitHub issues,
publish blog posts, transfer cryptocurrency, or make pledges on a user's
behalf.

Offers that require the worker to pay money, contain a negative/reverse
obligation, or otherwise conflict with the advertised reward are rejected.
Ambiguous financial or external-action instructions require explicit human
review. Credentials, wallet addresses, and secrets must not be entered into
the tool or committed to the repository.
